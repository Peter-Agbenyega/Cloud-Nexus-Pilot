# Product Requirements Document

## Product

Cloud Nexus Market

## Purpose

Ecommerce for cloud tools and digital products

## MVP Goals

A commerce-first storefront for cloud assets, digital bundles, and future downloadable product fulfillment.

## Primary User Outcomes

- Understand the offer within the first screenful.
- Navigate to the most important conversion pages quickly.
- Trust the product because the UI feels modern, polished, and intentional.

## MVP Scope





- Market homepage
- Categories overview
- Product listing
- Dynamic product detail template
- Downloads area
- Contact flow

## Non-Goals For MVP

- Payment processing
- Real authentication and user provisioning unless separately integrated
- Production analytics and CRM wiring
- Full CMS authoring

## Technical Notes

- App Router structure keeps marketing and product pages organized.
- Tailwind CSS is used for consistent, reusable UI patterns.
- Manual integration items are tracked in `docs/TASKS.md`.
